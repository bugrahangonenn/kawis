FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Who do you sell to?

Wholesalers, distributors, outlet and stock shops, and resellers. We don't sell to individual shoppers or in single pieces.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, we sell wholesale only. Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram and we'll tell you the current minimum order for the styles you want.

Why are there no prices on the website?

Stock changes daily and the unit price depends on your total quantity. Send us screenshots and estimated quantities and we'll quote for your volume.

Can I mix sizes and colours?

Yes. Mixed sizes and colours are possible within a lot.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. Samples are available before a bulk order. Samples are paid and the buyer pays sample shipping.

How long does shipping take, and what does it cost?

It depends on your country and the size of your order. Message us with your destination and we'll give you the details before you order.

How can I pay?

PayPal, Wise or Western Union.

What if there is a problem with my order?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll resolve it with you. Ordering a sample first is the best way to check quality before a bulk order.

How often do you add new stock?

Every day. New items are posted to our Telegram catalogue first.

Where are you based?

Kayseri, Türkiye. Kawis is a registered sole proprietorship. We work in English and Turkish, on Türkiye time (GMT+3).
